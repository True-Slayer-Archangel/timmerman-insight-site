Case study · sample build

Budget vs Actuals Tracker

An eleven tab Excel workbook, with a Google Sheets version, that turns a budget export and a raw GL export into spend against plan for every department and category.

Northwind Industries is a fictional company and every figure is illustrative. The plan runs January to December 2026, and the actuals run through June 30.

11
tabs, from Start Here to Engine
6
departments and 7 budget categories
214
GL lines and 22 budget lines
26
checks, all passing

How it works

It runs on two exports you already have.

The budget comes in with one row per department and category and twelve monthly amounts. The GL comes in with one row per transaction.

A Mappings table sends each GL account to its budget category, so a new account next month means one new row, not a broken formula.

Checks

26 checks run on every refresh.

Each one reads zero when the numbers tie. When one fails, it says where to look.

10
Reconciliations: totals that must agree across tabs.
11
Data quality counts: unreadable dates, unknown departments, unmapped accounts, duplicate IDs, and spend with no budget line.
2
Coverage tests: every new department and category reaches the views.
3
Capacity warnings before an input table fills up.
