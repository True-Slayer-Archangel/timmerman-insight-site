Alder Court Apartments

A fictional 100 unit garden community in ten two story buildings, bought for $17.5 million at the end of 2026, renovated one unit at a time as leases end, and sold after five years. Every figure here comes from the Multifamily Value Add Acquisition Model.

The plan

The seller has already renovated 8 units, and 2 stay classic. The other 90 are renovated as their leases end, starting in February 2027, with the crew starting up to six a month. Each one is offline for a month of work and vacant for a month of lease up, then leases at the renovated rent. The last renovation starts in April 2028, every unit is leased by June 2028, and at the busiest point 12 units are out of service.

The $1,064,000 budget is funded at the close and held in a reserve: $779,000 is spent in 2027 and $285,000 in 2028.
