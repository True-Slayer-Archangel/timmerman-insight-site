What I build

Most projects are one of these six.

Each one starts from your own exports and the way your team already works.

FP&A Dashboards

KPI and financial dashboards that refresh from your own data, with variances and trends laid out for the monthly review.

Excel & Sheets Automation

Power Query, VBA, and Apps Script for the jobs you redo every week. The report refreshes in a click instead of taking an afternoon of copy and paste.

Financial Models

Three statement models, SaaS forecasts, and scenarios, laid out so someone else can follow every number and change an assumption without breaking anything.

Budget vs Actuals

Trackers that compare spend with plan as soon as the GL export lands, and flag the lines running over before month end.

Excel Apps & Custom Tools

Workbooks with menus, forms, and buttons, so your team clicks through a quote, an invoice, or a schedule instead of editing formulas.

AI Assisted Finance Tools

Sheets that call Claude or OpenAI to categorize transactions, draft commentary, or summarize a month, right inside the workbook.

About

More than a hundred projects, mostly in Excel and Google Sheets.

I started freelancing after a few internships. The advice I kept hearing was to work for free until I had enough experience, and in this economy that didn't make sense to me, so I took the harder road and went straight to paid client work. Today I'm a Top Rated Plus freelancer on Upwork.

The part I enjoy most is working out what's slowing someone down and building the fix. Most of my clients are finance teams and founders who need a model, dashboard, or report they can put in front of a board or a lender, so I build each file to be checked: inputs in one place, formulas you can follow, and a tab that shows whether the numbers tie.
