What I build

Tools that take the manual work off your plate.

Every build is designed around your real data and how your team actually operates, not a rigid template you have to bend to fit.

FP&A Dashboards

Live KPI and financial dashboards that pull from your data and update automatically. Variance views, trends, and clean visuals stakeholders actually read.

Excel & Sheets Automation

Power Query, VBA, and Google Apps Script that remove repetitive work. Reports that refresh on a click instead of an afternoon of copy and paste.

Financial Models

Three statement models, SaaS forecasts, and scenario analysis. Clean, auditable, and easy to update as your assumptions change.

Budget vs Actuals

Variance trackers that flag overspend automatically, so you see what is off plan the moment the numbers land, not at month end.

Excel Apps & Custom Tools

Workbooks that run like software: menus, forms, and buttons your team clicks instead of formulas they edit. Quotes, invoices, CRMs, schedulers, and trackers.

AI Assisted Finance Tools

Spreadsheets connected to AI for drafting, categorizing, and summarizing inside the sheet. A practical edge, built on tools I have shipped.
